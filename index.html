Pixel astrophotography mode: minutes of stacked exposures on a tripod, far from city light. The Milky Way, a green night glow over the hills, stars through the leaves.

The phone that saw it all

The Pixel 4a went everywhere: ferry decks, metro escalators, hill trails at midnight. When the screen cracked, I fixed it. When it cracked again, I fixed it again. It was never just a phone; it was the notebook for an era of my life and my work.

In August 2026 it broke for the third time, and this time I couldn't afford the repair.
